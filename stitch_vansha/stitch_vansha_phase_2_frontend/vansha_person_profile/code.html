Eleanor Rose Vance

1924 — 2012

"The keeper of the coastal lighthouse and the stories that flickered within its beam."

Biography

Born in the coastal village of Ravenshear during the Great Autumn Gale of 1924, Eleanor was the daughter of a lighthouse keeper. Her early years were spent amidst the roar of the Atlantic and the rhythmic pulse of the Fresnel lens. She developed a profound love for literature and maritime history, often spending her afternoons cataloging the ship logs of her ancestors.

In 1946, she married Julian Vance, a scholar of ancient languages. Together, they established the Ravenshear Historical Society, dedicated to preserving the oral traditions of the coastal fishing families. Eleanor was known for her meticulous record-keeping and her uncanny ability to remember the birthday of every child born in the county.
